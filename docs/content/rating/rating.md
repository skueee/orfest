# Rating

Here are all the infos relative to how projects will be "payed out".

## Hours

The first way of getting points (and the most common one amongst YSWS) is with your coding hours.

You will get points depending on the number of hours you coded, registered with [Hackatime](https://hackcatime.hackclub.com). If your coding type is suspiciously high, it can be deflated.

## Titles and constraints

Don't you think that only accounting on hours is sometimes boring ?

This program introduces titles and constraints!

Basically, you can follow a constraint and/or make something very good at something to get more points!

### Constraints

Constraints are something you impose yourself to follow, a little challenge.

For example, it could be to use only the beautiful [Hack Club branded palette](https://hackclub.com/brand), or to hide AT LEAST 10 marvelous [Orpheus branded pictures](https://dino.icu/). Isn't that fun ? And what's funnier is that it will give you points !

At shipping, you will need to select every constraint you followed (and prove it).

The full explaination is available [here](/docs/rating/constraints).

### Titles

Titles are things you can earn after shipping, because your project did something very nicely!

For example, it could be loved by Hack Clubers, be very creative...

The full explaination is available [here](/docs/rating/titles).
