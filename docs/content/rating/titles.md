# Titles

Titles are one of the methods to get points at this YSWS!

## What are they?

Titles are honorative rewards given if your project does something very well, is very liked...

For example, you could get a title if the other Hack Clubbers really liked your project, or if your codebase is well documented.

## Selecting a title

You do not need to select most titles, they will be given out automatically.

However, some titles (ones that are harder to review) will need you to apply for them.
You will only be able to apply for a set number of them, so choose them wisely!

All the titles and their specifications will roll out in a list later on.

## Getting paid

Payout will roll out automatically whenever you get the titles, and will be usable as normal points!

The shop may be getting extended so that every title can be given out.
