# Start here

**Intrested in getting some merch ? Let me explain ya.**

The concept is pretty simple; you build something Hack Club related — Like a
game about Orpheus, a bot for Slack or even a tool to help other Hack Clubers —
and you get merch for it !

### Steps

**1. Build your project**

Build a project related to anything that rapproches to Hack Club ! Also, you
**can** choose a constraint (see below) to get mooore points.

**2. Ship it**

Send us your code, we'll review it and give you points.

**3. Shop !**

Get to choose between all the merch that we have to offer, and then wait for it
to get to your house.

### Constraints and titles

Don't you think that only accounting on hours is sometimes boring ?

This program adds two things; constraints and titles.

(PS: the list is in construction...)

But what are they ?

#### Constraints

Constraints are something you impose yourself to follow, a little challenge.

For example, it could be to use only the beautiful
[Hack Club branded palette](https://hackclub.com/brand), or to hide AT LEAST 10
marvelous [Orpheus branded pictures](https://dino.icu/). Isn't that fun ? And
what's funnier is that it will give you points !

At shipping, you will need to select every constraint you followed (and prove
it).

The full explaination is available [here](/docs/rating/constraints).

#### Titles

Titles are things you can earn after shipping, because your project did
something very nicely!

For example, it could be loved by Hack Clubers, be very creative... And of
course, you get points for it (yeah, i know, revolutianory type shi)

The full explaination is available [here](/docs/rating/titles).
