# Constraint

Constraint are one of the methods to get points at this YSWS!

## What are they?

A constraint is basically a little "challenge" that you need to complete in order to get points.

For example, I could ask you to put 10 different images of Orpheus in your project. If you decide to follow it, you will have to put them, add a proof when shipping, and you will get points for it!

## Choosing a constraint

To choose a constraint, there will be a list of the different constraints that you can follow. However, they are all optional, and you can even decide to follow none of them, as well as all of them. It's just a nice way to get points.

You can always draw back of a constraint while doing your project, because you only need to specify that you did them at shipping. If you did a constraint without knowing you were doing it, we wont know!

## Shipping a constraint

On the shipping form, you will be asked to select all the constraints you followed while making your project. You will need to enter proof for all of them, if applicable. For example, if you added 10 different Orpheus images, you will just select that constraint and add screenshots for the 10 Orpheus!
